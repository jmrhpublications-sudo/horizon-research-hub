JMRH Publications

For Authors

JMRH welcomes original, unpublished manuscripts that advance knowledge in any covered discipline. Follow the guidelines below to prepare your submission.

Manuscript Preparation

  • Use the JMRH Word/LaTeX template. Times New Roman 12 pt, 1.5 line spacing.
  • Structured abstract (150–250 words) and 4–6 keywords.
  • Sections: Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, References.
  • Citations in APA 7th edition (or as recommended by the section editor).

Submission Process

  1. Register an author account.
  2. Upload your manuscript and cover letter.
  3. Complete the conflict-of-interest and originality declaration.
  4. Track your submission through the dashboard.

Peer Review & Ethics

All manuscripts undergo double-blind peer review and plagiarism screening. JMRH follows COPE guidelines on publication ethics, authorship, and corrections.

Open Access

Accepted articles are published under Creative Commons (CC BY) licensing and freely available to readers worldwide.
