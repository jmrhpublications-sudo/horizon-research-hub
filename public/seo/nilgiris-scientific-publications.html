Nilgiris District Scientific Paper Publications

Last Updated: June 2026 HQ Location: Gudalur, Nilgiris, TN Peer Review: Double-Blind Refereeing Digital Preservation: PKP PN, LOCKSS

The Journal of Multidisciplinary Research Horizon (JMRH) is a key scientific publication channel in the Nilgiris district. We publish peer-reviewed scientific articles, tech studies, and botanical surveys from Ooty university scholars, Gudalur researchers, and national scientific societies. JMRH offers stable DOI citations and free PDF downloads.

Scientific Publishing Mission

Promoting open-access scientific publications across the Nilgiris district, helping researchers from local colleges (Ooty, Coonoor, Gudalur) publish peer-reviewed, DOI-indexed scientific studies without high fees.

Scientific Research Scope & Sections

JMRH curates and publishes research across major scientific branches. Here is a summary of our primary sections:

Scientific Branch Topics & Areas Welcomed
Life Sciences & Biology Western Ghats plant biodiversity, wildlife conservation, entomology, shola forest ecology, and ethnobotanical studies.
Applied Mathematics & Physics Mathematical modeling, high-altitude physics, environmental physics, climate projections, and data analysis.
Computer Sciences & IT Machine learning algorithms, software engineering, agricultural tech solutions, e-learning systems, and IoT.
Environmental Chemistry Soil composition in tea gardens, pesticide analysis, river basin water toxicity studies, and green agriculture chemistry.

Partnering with Local Colleges for Symposia

JMRH supports scientific development in Ooty and Gudalur. We collaborate with college departments to publish peer-reviewed conference proceedings. Every paper in a proceeding gets fully indexed with its own Crossref DOI and is added to the permanent LOCKSS digital archives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does JMRH publish conference proceedings from Nilgiris colleges?

Yes. JMRH regularly partners with colleges in Ooty, Coonoor, and Gudalur to publish peer-reviewed conference proceedings as special issues or regular volumes with individual DOI assignments.

Are LaTeX submissions accepted for scientific equations?

For initial screening, authors must submit a Microsoft Word document (.docx). However, JMRH supports LaTeX typesetting for final publications to render complex mathematical formulas and tables correctly in the PDF version.

What scientific disciplines are covered?

JMRH covers multiple scientific areas, including: Applied Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, computer systems, machine learning, ecology, biology, and agricultural sciences.
