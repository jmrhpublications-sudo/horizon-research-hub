JMRH Publications

Aims & Scope

JMRH publishes original research, review articles, case studies, and conceptual papers across a wide spectrum of disciplines, reflecting the multidisciplinary horizon of contemporary scholarship.

Disciplines Covered

  • Commerce, Management, and Business Studies
  • Economics, Banking, and Finance
  • Education, Pedagogy, and Psychology
  • Social Sciences and Humanities
  • Pure and Applied Sciences
  • Engineering, Technology, and Information Systems
  • Environmental Studies and Sustainability
  • Public Policy, Governance, and Law
  • Entrepreneurship and Innovation

Article Types

Original research articles, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, short communications, case reports, and editorials.
