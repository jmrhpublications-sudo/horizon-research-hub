Gudalur Environmental Research Papers & Nilgiris Biodiversity Articles

The Nilgiris and Gudalur region of Tamil Nadu lies within the Western Ghats – a UNESCO biodiversity hotspot. JMRH welcomes original environmental, ecological and biodiversity research from this fragile ecosystem.

Research areas

  • Forest ecology and wildlife conservation in the Nilgiris
  • Climate change impact on Western Ghats biodiversity
  • Sustainable agriculture and tea plantation studies in Gudalur & Ooty
  • Water resources, river ecology, soil and air quality
  • Indigenous community studies and conservation policy

Open access & citation-ready

Every accepted paper receives a permanent DOI, free PDF download and Creative Commons CC BY-NC licensing for maximum citation impact.
