The Journal of Multidisciplinary Research Horizon (JMRH) provides open access to peer-reviewed academic research across Tamil Nadu—focusing on Gudalur, Ooty, and the wider Nilgiris district. Every published article includes a unique DOI and is available as a free PDF download for immediate academic study.

Open Access PDF Delivery

Providing direct, unbarred access to research PDFs under CC BY-NC 4.0 terms, ensuring that students, professors, and field practitioners can download and utilize findings without subscription costs.

Promoting Scientific Visibility in South India

JMRH serves as a crucial digital archiving channel for research from colleges across Tamil Nadu. We invite academic departments organizing conferences in sciences, commerce, and education to publish their proceedings with us. All accepted papers are structured into downloadable PDF volumes, indexed on Google Scholar, and preserved permanently in lockss networks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I download PDF versions of JMRH articles?

All published articles are listed on the JMRH Archives page. Every paper has a dedicated download link for its full-text PDF document, accessible with no fees or registration requirements.

Can I republish or distribute JMRH PDF articles?

Yes. JMRH articles are published under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial (CC BY-NC 4.0) license. You are free to share, copy, and redistribute the material, provided you attribute the original authors and JMRH, and use it only for non-commercial purposes.

Is JMRH indexed in Google Scholar?

Yes, all JMRH publications and their PDFs are automatically crawled and indexed in Google Scholar, BASE, and academic directories to ensure maximum citation visibility.
