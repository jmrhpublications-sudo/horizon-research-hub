JMRH Publications

Research Paper Publications – Gudalur, Ooty & Tamil Nadu Academic Journals (PDF)

JMRH provides free PDF access to peer-reviewed academic research from across Tamil Nadu – with a strong focus on Gudalur, Ooty and the wider Nilgiris district. Every article is permanently archived under Creative Commons CC BY-NC.

Why JMRH?

  • Open Access: Free PDF download for every published article.
  • Peer Reviewed: Strict double-blind review by subject experts.
  • DOI Assigned: Citation-ready permanent identifier.
  • Monthly Publication: Fast turnaround for time-sensitive research.
  • Low APC: ₹650 only, paid after acceptance.

Disciplines covered

Commerce, economics, education, social sciences, science, technology, engineering, humanities, environmental studies and biodiversity – all available as downloadable PDFs from the JMRH archive.
