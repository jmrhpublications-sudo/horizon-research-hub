Editorial Board & Reviewer Community

JMRH is guided by a diverse Editorial Board composed of senior academics, researchers, and domain experts, supported by a global community of subject-matter reviewers.

Editorial Board

The Editorial Board sets editorial direction, oversees ethical standards, and ensures the quality and integrity of every issue. Members include Editor-in-Chief, Associate Editors, and Section Editors representing each discipline JMRH publishes.

Reviewer Board

Reviewers conduct double-blind evaluations of submitted manuscripts. JMRH welcomes new reviewers with research credentials and expertise in their fields. Apply to become a reviewer.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain confidentiality of submissions.
  • Provide constructive, evidence-based feedback.
  • Declare and avoid conflicts of interest.
  • Uphold COPE-aligned publication ethics.
