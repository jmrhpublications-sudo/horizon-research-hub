Editorial Board & Reviewer Community

Last Updated: June 2026 Ethics Standards: COPE Aligned Board Size: 25+ Editorial Experts Affiliations: National & International

The **Journal of Multidisciplinary Research Horizon (JMRH)** is directed by a dedicated Editorial Board. Our members are senior professors, research advisors, and subject specialists representing academic departments globally. Working alongside our editorial team, a diverse Reviewer Board ensures every published manuscript conforms to scientific and academic criteria.

Editorial Leadership Philosophy

Ensuring scientific rigor, transparency, and ethical publishing policies aligned with global COPE protocols, thereby safeguarding the integrity of our scholarly community.

Role Definitions & Ethical Duties

Members of our academic boards serve critical functions. They oversee peer review and guide the strategic growth of JMRH:

Board Section Duties & Ethical Standards
Editor-in-Chief & Editors Determine manuscript suitability, choose referees, resolve ethical issues, and oversee publishing timelines.
Reviewer Board (Referees) Perform double-blind peer-review assessments on scientific merits, methodology correctness, and citation accuracy.
Conflict of Interest Policies Editors and reviewers must declare any conflicts (financial, personal, or institutional) and recuse themselves immediately.
Reviewer Recognition We support ORCID integration, allowing reviewers to verify and document their editorial service details.

Upholding COPE Principles

JMRH is strictly aligned with the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) Core Practices. Board members participate in ongoing training to identify plagiarism, data falsification, and citation manipulation, ensuring our readers receive peer-reviewed studies of the highest caliber.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply to become a peer reviewer for JMRH?

Academic experts can apply by visiting the JMRH Reviewer Board page and submitting their CV, ORCID iD, and research interest keywords. Successful applicants must hold a post-graduate degree or PhD with publication records.

What are the ethical responsibilities of JMRH Board members?

All editors and reviewers must adhere to the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) guidelines. This includes maintaining absolute confidentiality, declaring conflicts of interest, and ensuring fair, unbiased evaluations.

Are review assignments credited or tracked?

Yes, JMRH supports peer review recognition. Reviewers can connect their ORCID profiles to automatically track and verify their contribution to our review board.
