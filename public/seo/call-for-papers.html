Call for Papers – 2026 Submissions Open

Last Updated: June 2026 Submission Cycle: Monthly / Rolling Basis First Decision: 3–6 Weeks ISSN Status: Applied For

The Journal of Multidisciplinary Research Horizon (JMRH) invites researchers, professors, postgraduate students, and doctoral scholars to submit original, unpublished manuscripts for our 2026 monthly volumes. We offer a double-blind peer-reviewed publishing workflow, low APC, and rapid online dissemination.

Call for Papers Focus

Providing a regular, month-by-month publishing channel to accelerate scientific dialogue and reward original empirical research with swift peer review and stable indexing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the deadlines for the upcoming issue?

JMRH publishes issues monthly. Submissions are accepted on a rolling basis. Manuscripts submitted before the 20th of any month are typically evaluated for inclusion in the following month's issue.

Can I submit my research before receiving my degree?

Yes, JMRH supports postgraduate and PhD candidates. Student research is welcomed, and our editors provide extra structural mentorship for student papers.

Are there formatting requirements for the initial draft?

Yes. Authors should use the JMRH template (Microsoft Word format) and use APA 7th edition citation style. Visit the Author Guidelines page for files and details.
