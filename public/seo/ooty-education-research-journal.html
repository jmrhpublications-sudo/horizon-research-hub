Ooty Education Research & Pedagogy Journal

Last Updated: June 2026 Scope: Pedagogy, Inclusive Education, Policy Target Region: Tamil Nadu & India Access: Diamond Open Access

The Journal of Multidisciplinary Research Horizon (JMRH) is a key publishing channel for education research, classroom pedagogy, and learning technologies. We serve as a vital platform for educational faculties at Ooty colleges, Gudalur teacher-training institutes, and universities across Tamil Nadu, offering professional referee reviews and stable indexing.

Educational Research Mandate

To support high-quality studies in teaching methodology, e-learning design, and inclusive policy frameworks, bridging the gap between local classroom research and global academic indexations.

Education Research Topics & Themes

JMRH welcomes empirical studies, reviews, and qualitative analyses across these major areas of interest:

Subject Section Scope and Regional Applications
Pedagogical Methods Active learning models, digital classroom implementations, curriculum designs, and assessment strategies.
Inclusive Education Tribal student enrollment, language barriers, special education resources in government schools, and rural education programs.
Teacher Education Professional development studies, B.Ed./M.Ed. curriculum review, teacher psychological stress, and pedagogical training results.
Educational Technology E-learning platforms, mobile learning tools, digital classrooms, and computer-assisted assessments.

Partnering with Local Colleges for Symposia

JMRH collaborates with schools and colleges of education in the Nilgiris to publish conference proceedings. If your institution is organizing a seminar on pedagogical reforms, JMRH offers special volume packages featuring individual DOI assignments and permanent PKP PN archiving for all accepted papers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does JMRH publish action research by school teachers?

Yes. JMRH actively supports the publication of classroom action research, pedagogical innovations, and case studies written by educators and school teachers in Ooty and across Tamil Nadu.

Can education papers focus on tribal learning in the Nilgiris?

Yes, studies on inclusive learning, multilingual education, and digital literacy in tribal schools of the Nilgiris are highly relevant to the JMRH multidisciplinary scope.

How long is the review process for education submissions?

The double-blind peer review for education and pedagogy manuscripts typically takes 3–5 weeks, with monthly publishing intervals.
