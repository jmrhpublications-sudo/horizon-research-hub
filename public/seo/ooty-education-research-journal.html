JMRH Publications

Ooty Education Research Publication Journal

JMRH serves as a dedicated platform for education research from Ooty colleges, Gudalur teacher-training institutes and Nilgiris universities. We publish peer-reviewed conference papers, action research, curriculum studies and policy analysis.

Education research themes

  • Higher education policy and reform in Tamil Nadu
  • Pedagogy, e-learning and digital classrooms
  • Teacher education and professional development
  • Tribal education and inclusive learning in the Nilgiris
  • Assessment, evaluation and educational psychology

For professors & faculty

Faculty members are invited to submit original education research. JMRH offers mentoring for first-time authors and a fast 3–4 week review cycle.
