About JMRH – Journal of Multidisciplinary Research Horizon

Last Updated: June 2026 ISSN (Online): Applied For Frequency: Monthly Publisher: JMRH Publications

The Journal of Multidisciplinary Research Horizon (JMRH) is a double-blind peer-reviewed, monthly open-access academic journal published by JMRH Publications. Headquartered in Gudalur, The Nilgiris, Tamil Nadu, India, the journal serves as a key multidisciplinary platform to advance scholarly investigations, theoretical critiques, and field studies across a broad range of scientific and academic disciplines.

What is JMRH?

A double-blind peer-reviewed, open-access journal fostering multidisciplinary scholarship and offering mentorship for first-time authors, particularly from rural and developing regions.

Our Core Mission

JMRH aims to bridge the gap between regional research communities and global academic discourse. We provide an inclusive, ethical, and highly professional platform for postgraduate scholars, doctoral candidates, assistant professors, and senior researchers across India and globally. A unique feature of JMRH is our commitment to providing constructive mentorship and editorial support to early-career researchers to help them align their submissions with international standards.

Rigorous Editorial Standards

To ensure academic excellence, every submission to JMRH undergoes a rigorous editorial lifecycle:

  • Double-Blind Peer Review: Every manuscript is evaluated by at least two independent referee experts in the relevant domain. Neither the author nor the reviewer knows the other's identity.
  • Plagiarism Control: Every manuscript undergoes screening for originality using industry-standard tools (like Crossref Similarity Check and iThenticate). The acceptable threshold is less than 10% similarity.
  • Creative Commons Licensing: Accepted papers are published under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial (CC BY-NC 4.0) license. Authors retain copyright while granting JMRH first publication rights.
  • Digital Preservation: Every published article is assigned a unique Crossref Digital Object Identifier (DOI) and is permanently archived under LOCKSS/CLOCKSS schemas and the PKP Preservation Network (PN) for permanent accessibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is JMRH an open-access journal?

Yes. JMRH is a fully open-access journal, meaning all articles are immediately and permanently free for anyone to read, download, and share under the CC BY-NC 4.0 license.

What is the Article Processing Charge (APC) at JMRH?

To support open-access publishing, JMRH charges a minimal APC of INR 650 for Indian authors and USD 10 for international authors. There are no submission fees; the charge applies only after acceptance.

How long does the review and publication process take?

JMRH follows a fast-track peer-review system. The editorial screening takes 3–5 days, and the double-blind review process takes typically 3–6 weeks. Upon acceptance, articles are published online within a week.
