About JMRH – Journal of Multidisciplinary Research Horizon

JMRH (Journal of Multidisciplinary Research Horizon) is a peer-reviewed, open-access academic journal published from Gudalur, The Nilgiris, Tamil Nadu, India. The journal advances scholarly research across commerce, management, education, science, technology, humanities, social sciences, and environmental studies.

Our Mission

To provide an inclusive, ethical, and rigorous publishing platform for postgraduate scholars, PhD researchers, and faculty across India and abroad. JMRH offers free mentoring and structured peer review for first-time authors.

Editorial Standards

  • Double-blind peer review by qualified academic referees.
  • Plagiarism screening on every submission.
  • Open access under Creative Commons licensing.
  • DOI assignment and permanent archiving.
