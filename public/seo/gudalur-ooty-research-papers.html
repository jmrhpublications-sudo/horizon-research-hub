Last Updated: June 2026 HQ Location: Gudalur, Nilgiris, Tamil Nadu Scope: Multidisciplinary Archiving: LOCKSS, CLOCKSS, PKP PN

The Journal of Multidisciplinary Research Horizon (JMRH) is a peer-reviewed, open-access academic journal serving researchers, faculty members, professors, and PhD scholars across India, with a deep connection to Gudalur, Ooty, and the Nilgiris district of Tamil Nadu. JMRH provides a professional platform for publishing original research articles, review papers, case studies, and conference proceedings.

Regional Publishing Mission

Empowering local educators, doctoral students, and scholars across Tamil Nadu with high-quality editorial mentoring, ensuring that unique regional data on ecology, education, and commerce reaches global visibility.

Why Publish with JMRH?

JMRH provides the structural tools of a global academic journal while maintaining local accessibility and support:

Journal Benefit Details & Specifications
Double-Blind Peer Review Evaluations managed by international referees, preserving author-referee anonymity.
DOI Indexing Every accepted article is indexed with a unique Crossref Digital Object Identifier.
Affordable APC Minimal processing fee of INR 650 for Indian authors to maintain open access.
Monthly Distribution Fast editorial workflows, ensuring online indexing within 3–6 weeks.
Author Mentorship Active support, editing assistance, and structure formatting guidelines for new scholars.

Fostering Academic Growth in the Nilgiris

We work closely with faculties and colleges in Ooty, Coonoor, and Gudalur, supporting conference paper collections and institutional proceedings. If your department or college is hosting a seminar in commerce, management, science, or humanities, JMRH offers special volume editions in PDF format with individual DOIs for all accepted papers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does JMRH offer support for first-time authors?

Yes. One of our core missions is mentoring early-career researchers, assistant professors, and PG students. JMRH provides constructive feedback and writing guidelines to help first-time authors refine their papers for international standards.

Can scholars from Ooty and Gudalur colleges submit papers?

Absolutely. JMRH actively collaborates with researchers, professors, and scholars from academic institutions across Ooty, Coonoor, Gudalur, and the wider Nilgiris district, providing a localized platform for globally relevant findings.

How do I submit my manuscript?

Manuscripts must be submitted through our online submit portal. Register an account, download our template, and upload your Word file for initial screening.
