Gudalur & Ooty Research Paper Publications – JMRH

JMRH (Journal of Multidisciplinary Research Horizon) is a peer-reviewed, open-access academic journal published from Gudalur, The Nilgiris, near Ooty in Tamil Nadu. We publish research articles, conference papers and review articles from professors, PhD scholars and college faculty across India.

Why publish with JMRH?

  • Double-blind peer review completed in 3–4 weeks.
  • Permanent DOI and free open-access PDF download.
  • Affordable APC – only ₹650 INR, charged after acceptance.
  • Coverage for commerce, education, science, technology, environmental and biodiversity research.
  • Rooted in the Nilgiris – supporting Ooty, Gudalur and Tamil Nadu scholarly community.
