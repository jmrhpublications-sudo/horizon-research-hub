Research Paper Publication in Gudalur, Ooty & Nilgiris – JMRH

The Journal of Multidisciplinary Research Horizon (JMRH) is a peer-reviewed, open-access academic journal serving researchers, faculty members, professors, and PhD scholars across India. Based in Gudalur, The Nilgiris, Tamil Nadu, JMRH provides a professional platform for publishing high-quality research articles, review papers, conference papers, and interdisciplinary studies.

Why Publish with JMRH?

  • Double-blind peer-review process.
  • DOI assignment for accepted papers.
  • Open-access publication with downloadable PDF articles.
  • Fast editorial and review workflow.
  • Affordable publication charges (APC).
  • Multidisciplinary coverage across science, education, commerce, technology, management, social sciences, and environmental studies.

Research Areas Covered

JMRH welcomes original research and review articles in education, science, technology, environmental studies, biodiversity, commerce, economics, management, humanities, social sciences, and interdisciplinary research. Researchers from Gudalur, Ooty, Nilgiris, Tamil Nadu, and across India are encouraged to submit their work.

Academic Publishing for Researchers and Faculty

Whether you are a professor, assistant professor, researcher, PhD scholar, postgraduate student, or conference presenter, JMRH offers a trusted platform for sharing scholarly work with a wider academic audience. Published articles are accessible online and contribute to knowledge dissemination through open-access publishing.

Submit Your Manuscript

Authors can submit original manuscripts, review papers, case studies, conference papers, and interdisciplinary research articles through the JMRH submission system. Each manuscript undergoes editorial screening and peer review before publication.
